This reference desk reads the World Register of Marine Species (WoRMS), maintained by expert editors and hosted at the Flanders Marine Institute (VLIZ).

How to read this record

Accepted is the register’s current taxonomic status. Synonyms and alternative representations connect other names to it; they are not a dated history of name changes.

Distributions are the locations reported in this register. They are not a complete range map or confirmation that a species is present today. Missing records do not mean absence.

Small captures, open searches

Saved names are dated examples, not the limit of this tool. Search any scientific name or AphiaID, download your records, or import a previous export. Nothing you search is stored in this browser after reloading.

Record-modified dates come from WoRMS. Retrieved dates say when a copy was collected. A fresh lookup may differ from a saved capture.

WoRMS Editorial Board (2026). World Register of Marine Species. Available at VLIZ. doi:10.14284/170. Individual taxon citations appear with each record. Text is reused under WoRMS’s attribution terms; this app does not redistribute the whole register.
